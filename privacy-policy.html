Snap & Snack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Snap & Snack ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information we collect

Snap & Snack does not ask for your name, email address, contacts or photos. Your game progress is saved on your device. Two optional or third-party services described below may process some data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a short ad after some completed levels, and optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward (extra shots or a life). To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), device information and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent first. Players in US states with privacy laws can opt out of the sale or sharing of their data for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy choices button in the game's pause menu. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Google Play Games sign-in (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your Play Games display name and player ID are used to save your game progress (levels, stars, coins, gems and characters) to your Google account, so you can continue on another device. This is stored by Google under your account (Google Play Games Saved Games). You can delete it from your Google account at any time. If you don't sign in, nothing is saved online.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (sound, music) are saved on your device. They are deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Purchases

The game has no real-money purchases. Coins and gems are earned in the game and have no real-world value.

Changes

If a future version changes what information is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please use the developer contact email shown on the Snap & Snack page on Google Play.
